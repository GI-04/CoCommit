## Privacy Policy

### 1. Who we are
CoCommit ("**CoCommit**", "**we**", "**us**", "**our**") is a collaboration service for software teams, available at https://cocommit.vercel.app and as a desktop app. CoCommit is operated by its founders, acting together as the data controller:

- Kotchamol Kamprasert
- Phisit Charoenkitpeeti
- Pattadon Yuttaworawit
- Trin Vejakron

(together, the "**CoCommit team**"). If CoCommit is later run by a registered company, that company will become the data controller and we will update this policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect personal data, in line with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) and its subordinate regulations (the "**PDPA**"). It applies to everyone who uses CoCommit ("**you**"), wherever you live. If you are outside Thailand, section 11 explains the extra rights you may have under your local law, including the EU and UK GDPR.

We do not currently have a designated Data Protection Officer. Send privacy questions and requests to any team member listed under [Contact](#contact).

### 2. Personal data we collect

**a. Data you give us**
- **Account data:** name, email address, password (stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider) and profile picture.
- **Content:** chat messages and pins, files you upload to Drive, tasks, team context, and the questions you ask the Assistant.
- **Communications:** messages you send us, such as support or privacy requests.

**b. Data we receive from services you connect**
- **GitHub:** when you sign in with GitHub, we receive your GitHub username, name, email address and profile picture, and an access token with the `repo` and `read:user` permissions. The `repo` permission lets CoCommit read and change repositories your GitHub account can access, including private ones. We use it only to perform the actions you request in CoCommit (for example, showing commits, committing, pushing, opening pull requests, uploading files and moving tasks).
- **Google:** if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture.

**c. Data collected automatically**
- **Technical and log data:** IP address, browser and device type, pages and features requested, dates and times, and error reports, collected by our hosting provider.
- **Cookies and browser storage:** see [Cookies and browser storage](#cookies-and-browser-storage).

**d. Calls**
Voice, video and screen sharing in Calls are transmitted in real time through our calling provider. **We do not record calls.**

**e. Sensitive personal data**
We do not ask for sensitive personal data (as defined in Section 26 of the PDPA, such as health, religion or criminal records). Please do not post it in CoCommit. If you do, you are choosing to disclose it to the people who can see that content.

### 3. Why we use personal data, and our legal basis

| Purpose | Data used | Legal basis under the PDPA |
|---|---|---|
| Create and manage your account; sign you in | Account data, GitHub/Google profile data | Performance of our contract with you |
| Provide CoCommit's features (chat, Drive, tasks, calls, repository actions) | Account data, content, GitHub access token | Performance of contract |
| Answer your Assistant questions | Your question and the repository content, pull requests, issues or chat needed to answer | Performance of contract |
| Keep CoCommit secure, prevent abuse, fix bugs | Technical and log data, account data | Legitimate interests (security and reliability of the service) |
| Respond to your requests and send essential service notices (for example, changes to these terms) | Account data, communications | Performance of contract; legal obligation |
| Meet legal obligations and handle legal claims | Any relevant data | Legal obligation; legitimate interests |

We do **not** sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use third-party analytics or tracking tools. If we ever want to use personal data for a purpose not listed here, we will tell you first and, where the PDPA requires it, ask for your consent.

If you do not provide the data needed for a feature (for example, if you do not connect GitHub), that feature will not work, but you may still use the rest of CoCommit where possible.

### 4. Who we share personal data with

- **Other CoCommit users.** Your display name, profile picture, and chat messages can be seen by other signed-in CoCommit users. Do not post passwords, API keys or other secrets in chat.
- **People with access to your repository.** Drive files, tasks and team context are stored in your GitHub repository (in the `COCOMMIT/` folder), so anyone who can access that repository can see them. If the repository is public, that content is public.
- **Service providers (data processors)** who run parts of CoCommit for us and may only use data to provide their service:

| Provider | What it does for CoCommit |
|---|---|
| GitHub, Inc. | Sign-in; repository access and storage for files and tasks |
| Supabase, Inc. | Sign-in, user profiles and chat database |

| LiveKit, Inc. | Real-time voice, video and screen sharing |
| Anthropic, PBC | AI processing for the Assistant |
| Google LLC | Sign-in with Google (only if you choose it) |

- **Authorities and legal requests**, when the law requires it or to protect the rights, safety or property of users, the public or the CoCommit team.
- **A successor**, if CoCommit is transferred to a company or another operator. We will tell you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

### 5. International transfers
Some of the providers above store or process data outside Thailand, including in the United States. We transfer personal data abroad only as permitted by Sections 28 and 29 of the PDPA, for example where the transfer is necessary to perform our contract with you, and where available we rely on providers' contractual data-protection commitments.

### 6. How long we keep personal data

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| Account and profile data | While your account is open. Deleted within 30 days after you ask us to close your account |
| Chat messages | While your account is open, or until you or we delete them |
| GitHub access token | In an encrypted cookie on your device for up to 30 days, or until you log out |
| Drive files, tasks, team context | In your GitHub repository until you delete them. Deleted files remain in the repository's git history, which you control |
| Assistant questions | Not stored by CoCommit after the answer is returned, unless you share the answer to chat. Anthropic processes them under its own terms |
| Technical logs | For the retention period set by our hosting provider |
| Records of privacy requests and legal matters | As long as needed to show we handled them, or as required by law |

Deleted data may remain in our providers' backups for a limited time before it is overwritten.

### 7. Your rights
Under the PDPA, you have the right to:
1. **Access** your personal data and get a copy (Section 30);
2. **Data portability:** receive your data in a commonly used format or have it sent to another controller, where technically possible (Section 31);
3. **Object** to our use of your data, including use based on legitimate interests (Section 32);
4. **Erasure:** ask us to delete or anonymise your data (Section 33);
5. **Restriction:** ask us to pause use of your data (Section 34);
6. **Rectification:** ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data (Section 35);
7. **Withdraw consent** at any time, where we rely on consent. This does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew;
8. **Complain** to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

To exercise a right, email any team member listed under [Contact](#contact). We may need to confirm your identity first. We will respond within **30 days** of receiving your request. We may refuse a request where the PDPA allows (for example, where we must keep data to comply with the law or to establish or defend legal claims), and we will explain why.

You can also remove CoCommit's access to GitHub at any time in GitHub under **Settings → Applications**.

### 8. Security
We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including HTTPS encryption in transit, an encrypted `httpOnly` cookie for your GitHub access token, database access rules that block signed-out visitors from reading chat, and keeping secret keys only on our servers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

**Data breaches.** If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the PDPC within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, unless the breach is unlikely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms. If the risk to you is high, we will also notify you without undue delay and tell you what you can do.

If you find a security problem, please report it to any team member listed under [Contact](#contact).

## Cookies and browser storage

CoCommit uses only cookies and browser storage that are **strictly necessary** to sign you in and to run features you use. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cc_github` | Cookie (encrypted, `httpOnly`) | Keeps your GitHub access token so CoCommit can act on your repositories | Up to 30 days |
| `cc_github_state` | Cookie (`httpOnly`) | Protects GitHub sign-in against forged requests | 10 minutes |
| Supabase session (`sb-…`) | Cookie | Keeps you signed in | Until you sign out or the session expires |
| CoCommit settings (picked repository, Home layout, tabs, Assistant settings) | Local storage | Remembers your preferences on this device | Until you clear it |
| Your own Claude API key (optional) | Session storage, or local storage if you choose to remember it | Lets the Assistant use your own key | Until you close the tab, or until you clear it |

If you choose to use your own Claude API key, it is kept on your device and sent with each Assistant request to our server, which passes it to Anthropic. We do not store it on our servers.

You can delete cookies and storage in your browser settings. If you block the necessary cookies, signing in will not work.
