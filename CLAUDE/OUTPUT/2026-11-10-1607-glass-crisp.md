**Date:** 2026-11-10 16:07 · **By:** Claude-Trin · **Result:** ✅ done

## Goal
Every glass element in the app and on the landing page (navigation, tab bars, pills, thumbs, glass buttons) looks crisper and sharper and moves faster.

## What changed
- `src/app/globals.css` — glass material: clearer fill, hairline rim (bright top lip, faint bottom catch, thin dark line outside), tight contact shadow, blur 20 → 10 px; a lip of light that turns toward the pointer (`--px / --py`); glass-only springs `--glass-spring` / `--glass-press`; thumbs 0.75 → 0.36 s, stretch 0.65 → 0.34 s and smaller, press 0.14 → 0.08 s in and 0.7 → 0.34 s out, pointer light 0.6 s → 0.12 s in / 0.3 s out. The blue button got the same crisp top line.
- `src/lib/glass-map.ts` — thinner rim (6-16 px) that bends harder, frost 12 → 8, sharp over the outer half of the rim.
- `src/components/GlassEffects.tsx` — writes `--px / --py`; lens refit after 60 ms; the rim mask is a multiply now, which fixes the middle of the glass never being frosted in Chromium.
- `tests/glass.test.mjs` — pins the thin rim, the light frost and the sharp outer half.
- LIB `figma-ui`, GOTCHAS, DECISIONS, LOG, HANDOFF.

## Verification

- Browser pane (Chromium, dev server, demo data), 1440 / 768 / 375 px: Home, Messages, Team, Drive, Repository, landing. Lenses fitted, no sideways scroll.
- Frost: `/home` at 375 px scrolled so card text sits under the top bar: the middle is blurred, the rim shows the bent backdrop; white logo readable over a white card. Same check with `data-refract` removed (the Safari / Firefox look).
