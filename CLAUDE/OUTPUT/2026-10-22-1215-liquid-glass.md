# Deliverable: Liquid glass 2 (light bending, pointer light, springy motion)

**Date:** 2026-10-22 12:15 · **By:** Claude-Trin · **Result:** ⚠️ partial (built and checked in Chromium; other browsers and devices not checked)
**Plan:** `CLAUDE/PLAN/2026-10-22-liquid-glass.md` · **Task:** none (asked in chat by Trin)

## Goal
Make the glass look and feel more like liquid glass: nicer motion and real light bending.

## What changed
- `src/app/globals.css`: shared glass material (sheen, top lip, light pool, rim, pointer highlight via `@property --lx --ly --lit`), canvas-coloured tint on bars and pills, spring easings (`linear()`), `.slide-thumb` / `.slide-clip` / `stretch-x|y`, squishy press, Chromium lens rule, reduced transparency.
- `src/lib/glass-map.ts` (new): `lensFit` and `lensMap`, the lens displacement map for a rounded rectangle.
- `src/components/GlassEffects.tsx` (new): pointer light and the Chromium-only SVG lens per glass size; mounted in `src/components/AppShell.tsx`.
- `src/components/ui.tsx` (`Tabs`), `src/components/LinkTabs.tsx`, `src/app/files/page.tsx`: spring + droplet thumb.
- `src/components/Nav.tsx`: one sliding glass thumb for the active page (`useSlidingThumb`).
- `tests/glass.test.mjs` (new): the lens map.

## Verification
- `npm test` → 242/242 pass (5 new in `tests/glass.test.mjs`); `npm run build` passes; `npm run typecheck` clean.
- `npm run dev`, Claude's browser pane (Chromium), fake mode:
  - Home, Drive, landing, Messages: every `.glass` / `.btn-glass` has `--lens` and a matching `<filter id="cc-lens-…">`.
  - Light bending: a striped `position:fixed` div under the phone top bar (375 px) shows stripes curving through the rounded ends with a frosted middle.
  - Pointer light: hovering the sidebar sets `--lx/--ly` and `--lit: 1`.
  - Tabs: clicking Home → Overview starts a 750 ms `linear()` spring on the thumb and a 650 ms `stretch-x` on its glass (origin = leading edge). Measured mid-move (Activity → Cards), the thumb overshoots 28 px and stretches 176 → 214 px wide, then settles. During an overshoot the track's `scrollWidth` stayed equal to its `clientWidth`.
  - Sidebar: Home → Agent Context slides the thumb 280 px with `stretch-y`. It is aligned on the tablet rail and in the phone menu; before it is measured, the active link keeps its own glass.
  - Landing header over the white hero demo: white text readable after the tint (estimated ~4.7:1).
  - No console errors; no sideways scroll at 1440 / 768 / 375 px.
  - `@property`, `linear()`, the lens rule, `.slide-clip`, and the reduced-transparency and reduced-motion rules are present in the built stylesheet.
- `lensMap` for the 254×844 sidebar runs in ~18 ms once per size; it matches a full per-pixel loop exactly.
