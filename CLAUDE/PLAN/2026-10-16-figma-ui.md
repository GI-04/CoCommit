# Plan: Put the Figma design into the app

## Why
The Figma file (`A5ao1epDSl3hnbGX599un3`, page "test 4") is the agreed design, but the app still has the plain starter look (light/dark system colours, white cards, "Coming soon" style nav). Trin wants the app itself to look and work like the design, on desktop, tablet and phone, so the back-end team integrates against a real, usable app. Today only the email/Forgot/reset screens carry the Figma copy; none carry the Figma look.

## What I found
- Every page styles itself through seven colour tokens in `src/app/globals.css` (`bg`, `surface`, `fg`, `muted`, `line`, `accent`, `added`/`removed`) and the shared pieces `Nav.tsx`, `AppShell.tsx`, `ui.tsx`. **Changing the tokens re-skins all 9 pages at once**, with no page rewrites. No hard-coded colours were seen in a first scan (re-check while building).
- The app follows the system light/dark setting; the Figma is dark only.
- The landing page `/` has its own look (Schibsted Grotesk); it stays as is except where noted.
- **Real data exists** for: commit activity (heat map), tasks (`COCOMMIT/BOARD`), recent activity (`recentActivity`), online teammates (chat). It does **not** exist for: open-PR count, "Calendar", "Quick access" folders, sticky notes, Assistant window, per-person conversations. Those are not faked (see Phase 2).

## Approach (no new packages; fonts come from `next/font/google`, already used on `/`)
**Phase 1: the look, everywhere (this plan)**
1. **Tokens** in `globals.css` from the Figma variables: canvas `#1f1f1f`, ink `#000`, white `#fff`, paper `#fffdf8`, brand blue `#1f4ae0` (accent), pink `#ff79bf`, cyan `#00a9d8`, mustard `#d8a500`, green `#68ca27`, clay `#e07a5f` (errors/destructive). Dark only. Add `--color-pink/cyan/mustard/green` to `@theme`.
2. **Fonts:** Inter (Regular/Medium/Semi Bold) and JetBrains Mono, loaded in `layout.tsx`; type scale from the Figma text styles (Title/28, Heading/18/16/14, Body/14, Small/12, Label/11).
3. **Navigation in three sizes** (Figma "Sidebar", "Rail", "Top bar"): glass sidebar on desktop, icon rail on tablet, top bar with menu button on phone. Same links and routes as today; icons are inline SVG (no icon package).
4. **Shared pieces** (`ui.tsx`: `Button`, `Card`, `PageHeader`; new `Tabs`, `Pill`/`Avatar` styling): chunky coloured cards with a black outline, paper-white inner rows, black primary button, glass tab pills.
5. **Auth screens** (`/login`, `/login/forgot`, `/login/reset`, the email form): the coloured card with big title, round fields and Back / Next buttons from "Auth · …" frames, incl. phone width.
6. **Per-page pass** on Repo, Chat, Context, Calls, Tasks, Files, Profile: only what the new tokens and components do not fix by themselves (spacing, headers, tab bars), page by page, checked at phone / tablet / desktop widths.

**Phase 2: Home (same plan, after Phase 1 is checked)**
7. `/home` gets the Cards / Overview / Activity tabs. **Overview** = real commit heat map (exists), tasks-done count and Team board from `/tasks` data, online teammates from chat. **Activity** = `recentActivity(repo)`. **Customize** (hide/reorder cards) saved in `localStorage`, per device.
8. Cards with no data source today (open PRs, Calendar, Quick access, Sticky note, Work-done chart) are **left out and listed in the PR**, not mocked.

**Out of scope (separate plans if wanted):** merging Chat / Tasks / Calls into one `/messages` page with tabs (changes routes), the floating Assistant window (the `@cocommit` chat bot stays), Drive's Personal/Shared tabs, a restyle of the landing page, light mode.
