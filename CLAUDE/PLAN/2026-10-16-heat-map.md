# Plan: Commit activity heat map

**Status:** active (approved by Pattadon in chat, 2026-10-16; PR opened, not merged)
**Date:** 2026-10-16 · **Superseded by:** — · **Owner:** Claude-Pattadon (requested by Pattadon; chose "commit activity" over file hotspots / task activity)

## Why
Small teams can't see at a glance who is committing and when. A GitHub-style grid (day × intensity, filterable per person) makes the team's rhythm visible on the home screen and is demo-friendly.

## Approach
1. `src/lib/heatmap.ts` (pure, no imports from `github.ts`): `buildHeatMap(commits, weeks, now)` → weeks×7 cells `{ date, count }` per author and total, a 0–4 intensity level per cell, and the author list. Local-day bucketing.
2. `src/lib/github.ts`: new `listCommitDates(repo, since)` — default branch, `since` = start of window (client sends it), paginates `per_page=100` (max 5 pages), returns `{ author, at }[]`. Fake mode returns the fake commits. Add it to `actions` and `github-types.ts` / `github-client.ts`.
3. `src/components/HeatMap.tsx` (client): the grid (13 weeks by default), person filter, legend, hover/tap tooltip with date and count, "no commits yet" and "log in" states; works at phone width.
4. Show it on `/home` under the section cards (the one existing-page edit).
5. `tests/heatmap.test.mjs` for bucketing, levels, empty input, window edges.
